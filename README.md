# Crimson Nights

*The Crimson Museum*: a gothic, first-person vampire-hunting action game, made in Unity for spooky season.

You're a hunter sent to a Victorian mansion that has been turned into a private museum of vampire history. The exhibits are meant to be historical artefacts, but some of them are still alive. Fight through the galleries with a revolver, a relic shotgun and your fists, dodge at the last moment to turn an attack around, and find out what the curator has been keeping in the Archive.

The full design is in [`gameplan.md`](gameplan.md).

## Status

**Phase 4: vertical slice, in progress.** The slice is level 1 of a planned three, a 10–20 minute demo:

- **Entrance corridor and Grand Gallery**: the museum's hub, and the first ambush
- **Vampire History Wing**: the Morvayne family tomb, a coffin puzzle for the Archive Key, and a coffin that isn't empty
- **The Archive**: the curator's ledgers reveal what the exhibits really are, then the mini-boss, the Countess Morvayne
- **End of the demo**: a stats card with your time, deaths and best style rank

Each area has its own music, which drops out when a fight starts. Still to come for the slice: visual polish, and a title screen and pause menu.

## How to fight

- **Watch the telegraphs.** Vampires glow before they attack: **yellow** is a close-range strike, **red-orange** is a lunge from a few metres away. A thrall shrieks as it winds up a lunge, so listen for one coming from behind.
- **Perfect dodge.** Dodge just as an attack lands to slow time, stagger the attacker and open a counter window where your hits deal extra damage.
- **Stagger.** Light hits (a revolver shot, a jab) interrupt a strike. A lunge needs something heavier: a hook, a haymaker, or a point-blank shotgun blast.
- **Punch combos.** Tap for jab, cross, hook. Hold for a haymaker.
- **Style.** The meter on the right ranks you from D to SSS. Varying your attacks, switching weapons mid-fight, finishers and perfect dodges raise it. Getting hit and standing still drain it.
- **Supplies.** Every kill drops ammo or health, more likely whichever you're short of.

## Getting started (development)

**Requirements:** Unity **6000.3.6f1** (Unity 6) and [Git LFS](https://git-lfs.com/). Audio, textures, models and fonts are stored in LFS.

```sh
git lfs install
git clone https://github.com/ChrisOspina/Crimson-Nights.git
```

Open the folder in Unity Hub, then open `Assets/Scenes/Museum.unity` and press Play.

**Scenes**

- `Museum.unity`: the demo, first in Build Settings
- `Level1.unity`: a combat test arena with endless waves

**Both scenes are generated by editor tools.** Use **Tools ▸ Crimson Nights**:

- **Build Prototype Arena** makes the player setup, weapon and enemy data, enemy and pickup prefabs, and the arena. Run it once first.
- **Build Museum** makes the museum scene, including the Countess.

Re-running a builder rebuilds its scene. To change a layout, edit the builder (`Assets/Scripts/Editor/`) rather than the scene, or the next run will wipe your edits. Data assets (weapon, enemy and style tuning under `Assets/Data/`) are made once and then left alone, so tune them in the Inspector.

**Key packages:** URP 17.3, Input System 1.18 (new Input System only), Cinemachine 3, AI Navigation 2.
